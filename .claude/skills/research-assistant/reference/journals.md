# Choosing a journal

**Nothing in the starter list is verified for the current year.** HEC re-categorises journals (W, X, Y)
every year in **HJRS** (https://hjrs.hec.gov.pk), and Scopus / Web of Science coverage changes. Before
recommending a journal, check: the current HJRS category, the journal's own official website (beware hijacked
copies), the scope, recent issues, the review time, the fees, and the author guidelines.

## How to vet

1. HJRS category (W highest, then X, then Y) and the year it applies to.
2. Scopus: https://www.scopus.com/sources (or the Scopus source list xlsx); WoS: https://mjl.clarivate.com.
3. The site's URL matches the one listed in HJRS / Scopus (hijacked journals copy names).
4. Recent issues: are papers like ours published? Read 2–3 and copy their structure and citation style.
5. Turnaround and fees (APC). Some HEC journals charge a processing fee; very high fees with fast
   acceptance are a warning sign.
6. Language: Urdu, English, Arabic accepted?

## Starter list (to verify; names only, categories intentionally not stated)

- *Islamic Studies* (Islamic Research Institute, IIUI) — English, also Scopus-indexed historically
- *Fikr-o-Nazar* (IRI, IIUI) — Urdu
- *Al-Adwa* (Shaykh Zayed Islamic Centre, University of the Punjab)
- *Al-Qalam* (Institute of Islamic Studies, University of the Punjab)
- *Journal of Islamic Thought and Civilization (JITC)* (University of Management and Technology, Lahore)
- *Al-Basirah* (NUML), *Al-Duhaa*, *Al-Milal*, *Al-Az̤vā*, *Maʿārif-e-Islāmī* (AIOU), *Al-Īqāẓ*,
  *Al-Hikmah*, *Hamdard Islamicus* (Hamdard Foundation, Karachi), *Al-Tafseer*, *Al-Aijaz*, *Al-Qamar*

International (higher bar, longer review; English):
- *Journal of Islamic Studies* (Oxford), *Islamic Law and Society* (Brill), *Journal of Qurʾanic Studies*
  (Edinburgh UP), *Arab Law Quarterly* (Brill), *The Muslim World* (Wiley), *Islam and Christian–Muslim
  Relations* (T&F), *American Journal of Islam and Society*, *Religions* (MDPI, open access, APC),

Match the paper to the journal: textual / classical → JIS, ILS, JQS; contemporary application → Religions,
HTS, AJIS, JIE; Pakistan-focused → JITC, Islamic Studies, Al-Adwa.

## Journal checklist (fill in `articles/<slug>/journal.md`)

- [ ] Name, official URL, HJRS category + year / Scopus / WoS
- [ ] Scope fits; 2 recent similar papers read
- [ ] Word limit, abstract limit, keywords count
- [ ] Language; second abstract needed?
- [ ] Citation style; romanised references needed?
- [ ] Anonymised manuscript + separate title page?
- [ ] Fee (APC / processing), review time
- [ ] Declarations: originality, conflict of interest, AI use, funding, ethics
- [ ] Similarity limit
- [ ] Submission system (OJS / ScholarOne / email), ORCID
