# Researcher metrics, ranking lists and profiles

Use this when the user asks about citations, h-index, "top scientists" lists or university rankings, and
when planning the long-term profile. Methodologies change yearly: confirm details on the official
methodology pages before quoting numbers.

## Metrics

| Metric | Meaning | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Citations | how many times other works cite yours | the base of almost every ranking |
| h-index | h papers each cited at least h times | rewards steady, cited output; differs by database |
| i10-index | papers with ≥ 10 citations | Google Scholar only |
| Field-normalised (FWCI etc.) | citations vs. the world average of the same field and year | fairest across fields |
| Self-citation | citing your own work | ranking lists often exclude or penalise it |

The same person has different numbers in **Google Scholar** (widest: books, Urdu journals, theses),
**Scopus** (Elsevier; used by THE, QS and the Stanford list) and **Web of Science** (Clarivate; used by
Highly Cited Researchers). Humanities and Islamic studies have far lower citation rates than medicine or
engineering, and books count heavily; compare only within the field.

## Ranking lists

| List | Data | Humanities / Islamic studies? |
|---|---|---|
| AD Scientific Index | Google Scholar profiles (h-index, i10, citations, total and last 6 years); ranks by world, country, university, subject | yes, social sciences and humanities are included; only researchers with a public Google Scholar profile |
| Stanford / Elsevier "Top 2% Scientists" (Ioannidis) | Scopus, composite score; career-long and single-year lists, by 22 fields / 174 subfields | yes in principle (Arts & Humanities fields incl. religions & theology), but needs substantial Scopus-indexed output |
| Clarivate Highly Cited Researchers | Web of Science, top 1% cited papers in ESI fields | effectively no: arts & humanities are not ESI fields |
| Research.com, ScholarGPS | own databases | mainly sciences; check the discipline list |
| Pakistan: HEC / PCST | HEC uses HJRS-recognised publications for hiring and promotion; PCST ranks "productive scientists" | check whether the current PCST cycle covers social sciences / humanities |

## University and country rankings

THE and QS take research output and citations from **Scopus**; Nature Index covers only natural sciences.
A paper helps the university only if it is indexed and the **affiliation is written exactly** in the
standard English form. HEC also ranks Pakistani universities with its own criteria.

## Profile setup (do once, keep updated)

ORCID → Google Scholar (public, verified university email if possible) → ResearchGate / Academia.edu →
Scopus Author ID (created automatically after the first Scopus paper; merge duplicates) → one consistent
name spelling ("Zabeehullah Leghari") on every paper.

## What actually raises citations in this field

Papers others need to cite: review articles that map a field, new sources or editions, clear frameworks,
English-language work in Scopus/WoS journals, open access with a DOI, informative titles and abstracts,
sharing on the profiles above, and co-authorship with active researchers. Never citation cartels,
excessive self-citation or paid citations: ranking lists detect and penalise them.
