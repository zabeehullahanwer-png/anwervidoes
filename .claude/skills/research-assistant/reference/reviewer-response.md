# Cover letter and response to reviewers

## Cover letter (one page)

Dear Editor,

I am pleased to submit the manuscript "[Title]" for consideration in *[Journal]*. The article [one
sentence: question and contribution]. It addresses [gap], drawing on [sources/method], and finds that
[main finding]. It fits the journal's interest in [scope point, ideally linked to a recent paper in the
journal].

The manuscript is original, has not been published, and is not under consideration elsewhere. [It is
derived from the author's M.Phil thesis, *...*, University, year.] [AI-use statement if required.]
There is no conflict of interest.

Sincerely, [Name, affiliation, email, ORCID]

## Response to reviewers

Thank the editor and reviewers once, then a table per reviewer:

| # | Reviewer comment (verbatim) | Response | Change in manuscript (section / page / line) |
|---|---|---|---|
| R1.1 | ... | We agree and have ... | Section 3, p. 7, lines 120–135 |

Rules:
- Answer every point; quote it exactly.
- Agree where they are right and show the change. Where you disagree, give evidence politely
  ("We respectfully maintain ... because ... [source]"), and still make a small clarifying change.
- Mark changes in the revised manuscript (track changes or highlighted text) if the journal asks.
- Never argue with tone; never ignore a comment.
- Keep a clean version and a marked version.
