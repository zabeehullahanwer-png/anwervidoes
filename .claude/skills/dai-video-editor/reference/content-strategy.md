# Content strategy (from the Oct 2026 landscape research)

Full Urdu report with sources: `reports/اسلامی مواد کا منظرنامہ اور مواقع.md` (notes in `research_notes/`).
Read this file when planning a series, choosing what to cut from a raw video, or writing titles,
captions and descriptions. Findings marked *inference* rest on what searches did not find.

## Positioning the user owns
**«سند کے ساتھ: محقق، مناظر نہیں»**, a sourced researcher and not a debater: an Urdu aalim who says
everything with its reference. He brings the Dars-e-Nizami tradition and university research, and he
keeps adab in disagreement.
- **Sanad:** every ayah shows surah:ayah on screen. Every hadith shows its book, its number and the
  muhaddith's grading. Each video gets a page on zabeehullahleghari.com with the full takhrij, plus a
  public corrections log (اصلاح نامہ).
- **Bridge:** Urdu first, English subtitles always, and selected English reels for the diaspora.
  Pakistani CPM is about $0.42, against roughly $12 in the US.
- **Adab:** never refute a maslak, group or person by name. Present "the views of the scholars and their
  evidence". Saying "it is unknown" or "there is disagreement" is allowed.
- Never claim to be "the first" or "the only".
- In bios and titles, state only verified credentials: Dars-e-Nizami, an MA in Islamic Studies
  (University of the Punjab, 2021–23) and an MPhil (The Islamia University of Bahawalpur, 2024–26).
  **Do not say PhD** unless the user confirms it.

## Why (evidence in brief)
- **Urdu market:** large preachers dominate, mostly with raw lecture footage. Fan clip channels often
  out-reach the official ones. No calm, referenced, non-sectarian Urdu creator who shows references on
  screen was found (*inference*).
- **English market:** institutes like Yaqeen turn one research paper into animation, podcast and
  infographic, and run 30–31 episode Ramadan series. Debate channels grow fast but carry a
  "manosphere" stigma.
- **Platforms:**
  - FB and TikTok give the reach in Pakistan; YouTube gives search, depth and money.
  - Shorts and Reels can run up to about 3 minutes.
  - Signals: "Viewed vs Swiped Away" (the hook), average % viewed, and sends per reach (Instagram).
  - Each clip must add its own point; near-duplicates are penalised (YouTube inauthentic-content
    policy, Meta originality rules).
- **Audience:** youth rarely leave Islam but hide their questions: hadith doubts, crypto/riba, AI "muftis",
  mental health. They are tired of harsh tone, sectarianism and "celebrity scholars". Fabricated hadith
  spread widely.
- **Legal (Pakistan):** Engineer Mirza's 2025 case (295-C and PECA s.11) began with a viral interview
  clip. PECA s.26A ("false information") carries up to 3 years in prison. Calm sourcing is protection as
  well as scholarship.

### Editing implications for this engine
- **Reference card on every video:** use a `quote_card` with Arabic, translation and a reference line
  for the source, and end with a reference card plus the website URL.
- **Hadith Check:**
  - Show the "viral claim" as a headline in quotes, with a neutral treatment and no mocking.
  - Show the grading as a clear text overlay: صحیح / حسن / ضعیف / موضوع, with the muhaddith's name.
- **Maps and isnad graphs:** use aniconic visuals only (geometry, light, architecture, maps,
  manuscripts). Never depict prophets, sahaba or people. No realistic AI imagery.
- **Clips:** every clip must stand alone without its meaning changing (the context check). Keep the raw
  recording archived to prove context.
- **Hooks:** open on the question, phrased truthfully. No clickbait; YouTube's Test & Compare picks winners
  by watch time.
- **Sound:** no instrumental music. Use only the user's own recitation, or licensed recitation with credit
  (Content ID).

## Never
- Refute a maslak, group or person by name, and never cut a polemical clip.
- Take up contested topics about the Sahaba or Ahl al-Bayt.
- Make derisive comparisons with other religions' sacred figures or books.
- Publish an unverified ayah or hadith, or news, rumours and political claims (PECA 26A).
- Use a realistic AI likeness or a synthetic voice of the user without a visible AI label. Under the
  house rule, never use one at all for Qur'an or Hadith text.
- Re-upload others' content or produce near-duplicate template spam.
- Make demeaning generalisations about women or any group, or use a "celebrity" tone.

## Pre-publish check (every video)
1. **Text:** the ayah or hadith text, its number and its grading are verified by a second person.
2. **Sensitivity:** check the topic against the written list of topics to avoid.
3. **Context:** watched alone, the clip still means what was meant.

## Measure
- **Hook:** Viewed vs Swiped Away, against your own median.
- **Body:** average % viewed.
- **Spread:** sends per reach.
- **Trust:** clicks to the takhrij pages, the number of anonymous questions, and corrections logged.
- At day 45 and day 90, keep only the formats above median on both hook and retention.
