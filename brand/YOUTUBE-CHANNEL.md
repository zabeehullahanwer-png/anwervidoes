## 6. Video building blocks

- **Intro (16:9):** `intro_navy_16x9.mp4`, 6 seconds, for long videos.
- **Intro (9:16):** use the reel intro for Shorts, or start straight with the hook (Shorts work best
  without an intro).
- **Thumbnail:** `youtube_thumbnail_sample.jpg` is the template. Gold pill = series name, two short
  Nastaliq lines = the title (4–7 words), portrait on the right, logo bottom-left.
- **End screen:** leave the last 10–20 s of each long video calm (no text) so YouTube's end-screen
  elements (subscribe + next video) fit.
- **AI content:** if any video uses an AI voice or AI-generated likeness, tick *Altered or synthetic
  content* when uploading.
