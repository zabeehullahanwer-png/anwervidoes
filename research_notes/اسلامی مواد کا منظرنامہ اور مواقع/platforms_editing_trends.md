# Platform dynamics, audience data and editing trends for Islamic and educational creators (2025–2026), focusing on Pakistani/Urdu and English-speaking Muslim audiences

> Research date: 22 Oct 2026. Method: web search, with summaries checked against the snippets the search returned. DataReportal, PhoneWorld and Profit (Pakistan Today) pages could not be fetched directly: the proxy blocked them or DNS failed. Figures from those sources come from search-result snippets or secondary articles that cite them, and each is marked where it is used. Most platform "algorithm" guidance comes from third-party creator blogs rather than platform documentation, and the notes flag this.

## 1. Audience size and usage: Pakistan, the Urdu diaspora and English-speaking Muslim audiences

### Takeaway
Pakistan has roughly 117M internet users and about 80M social-media user identities (Digital 2026, via secondary citation). By ad reach, TikTok (about 67M) and Facebook (about 49–68M depending on the source and date) are the largest platforms, and YouTube's ad reach was about 56M in early 2025. Around 140M Pakistanis are still offline. Pakistani ad rates are among the lowest in the world, so diaspora viewers (Gulf, UK, North America) and India's Urdu/Hindi speakers matter much more for revenue than their numbers suggest.

This is secondary; the DataReportal page itself could not be opened. — [CollabsCafe, Pakistan Creator Economy 2026](https://collabscafe.com/blog/state-of-pakistan-creator-economy-2026); report landing page: [DataReportal – Digital in Pakistan](https://datareportal.com/digital-in-pakistan)

- Urdu has about **246M total speakers**: about 78M first-language (L1) and about 168M second-language (L2), counted over 2011–2023. Formal Urdu draws heavily on Arabic and Persian vocabulary. — [Wikipedia, Urdu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urdu)
- **India's 2011 census counted about 50.8M people whose mother tongue is Urdu**, making it India's 7th-largest language. — [Wikipedia, Urdu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urdu)

**English-speaking Muslim creators (anecdotal benchmarks)**

- Shoaib Hussain ("the Sunnah Guy") has over 2.9M Instagram followers and is described as the producer behind some of the most viral Islamic reels. ### Inferences
- Pakistani reach is large but earns little per view. A Pakistani channel that relies on AdSense needs very high volume. Diaspora audiences in the Gulf, UK and North America, plus India's Urdu/Hindi viewers, are worth far more per view than the Pakistani base alone.
- For a Pakistani da'i, TikTok and Facebook are likely the biggest reach channels by sheer audience, and YouTube is the most dependable route to revenue (see section 4 on Facebook and TikTok monetisation limits).
- X was unavailable in Pakistan for most of 2024–25 and remains a minor platform there. Its value is mainly for reaching English-speaking diaspora and journalist audiences, not mass Pakistani reach.

### Gaps
- The DataReportal Digital 2026 Pakistan per-platform figures could not be checked directly because the site was blocked. The 79.9M, 117M and 66.9M numbers are secondhand. The 66.9M TikTok figure appears in both the January 2025 and the 2026 citations, so the "2026" attribution may actually be the 2025 number.
- A search summary attributed a figure of about 54M for YouTube ad reach in Pakistan (October 2026) to Pakistani trade press. The article could not be opened, so treat it as unverified.
- No reliable data was found on the size of the Urdu-speaking diaspora on each platform, or on how large English-speaking Muslim audiences are on each platform.
- No 2025–26 industry study was found that measures the overall growth or reach of Islamic content categories on YouTube, TikTok or Meta.

## 2. What platform guidelines and creator research say: retention, lengths, hooks, captions, thumbnails, cadence, collaborations, series

### Takeaway
All platforms now let short-form video run up to about 3 minutes. Ranking rewards retention, meaning the share of the video watched plus absolute seconds watched, and shares/sends rather than likes. YouTube now counts every Short start as a view, but pays and judges monetisation eligibility on "engaged views". The "Viewed vs Swiped Away" metric is the practical hook test. YouTube's built-in A/B testing (titles and thumbnails, long-form only) picks winners by watch-time share, not CTR. Facebook Reels and TikTok's Creator Rewards favour original videos longer than 1 minute. Third-party data says Shorts of about 50–60 seconds collect the most views, but no single "optimal length" is supported.

### Cited Findings
**YouTube Shorts and long-form**
- **Shorts can run up to 3 minutes since 15 Oct 2024**, up from 60 seconds. — [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/youtube-changes-how-shorts-views-are-counted-from-march-31/); [TubeBuddy](https://www.tubebuddy.com/blog/youtube-shorts-view-count-update-what-creators-need-to-know-about-the-new-metrics/)
- **Since 31 Mar 2025, every start or replay of a Short counts as a view**, with no minimum watch time. The old metric survives as **"engaged views"** in Advanced analytics and still drives Shorts revenue sharing and YPP eligibility. — [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/youtube-changes-how-shorts-views-are-counted-from-march-31/); [TubeBuddy](https://www.tubebuddy.com/blog/youtube-shorts-view-count-update-what-creators-need-to-know-about-the-new-metrics/)
- *Unverified:* several 2026 blogs say YouTube extended start-based view counting to all formats on 24 Aug 2026. No official YouTube source confirmed this. — [Exemplary.ai](https://exemplary.ai/blog/youtube-views-vs-engaged-views); [Gyre](https://gyre.pro/blog/understanding-the-youtube-shorts-algorithm-a--guide)
- **"Viewed vs Swiped Away"** in Studio's Shorts analytics measures the share of feed viewers who stopped instead of scrolling past. Guides treat it as a "hook health" metric. Suggested benchmarks of 70–80% "viewed" are unofficial, and no official threshold exists. A strong Viewed rate combined with a low average percentage viewed means the hook worked but the body of the video did not. — [Subscribr](https://subscribr.ai/youtube-strategy/youtube-shorts-analytics-metrics-viral); [ByteCap research](https://www.bytecap.io/research/youtube-shorts-retention-benchmarks)
- **Length vs views:** an Inflow Network analysis of 5,400 Shorts, cited in vendor blogs, found that **50–60 s Shorts drew about 22 times more views than Shorts under 10 s**, even though percentage retention falls as length rises. Most top-performing Shorts are still 15–60 s, and retention benchmarks for 30–60 s Shorts conflict between sources (40–55% in one, 75–85% in another). — [Piktochart](https://piktochart.com/blog/how-long-youtube-shorts/); [OpusClip blog](https://www.opus.pro/blog/ideal-youtube-shorts-length-format-retention); [Retensis](https://retensis.com/blog/audience-retention-benchmarks-2026)
- Guides agree that the algorithm does not penalise longer Shorts by default. It penalises early drop-off. — [Virvid](https://virvid.ai/blog/best-shorts-length-retention-2026)
- **YouTube "Test & Compare":** creators can test up to **3 titles, 3 thumbnails, or title+thumbnail combinations** for up to 2 weeks. **The winner is chosen by watch-time share, not CTR.** It works on desktop only, for long-form videos only, and needs Advanced Features. Title testing reached all eligible creators around Dec 2025, and it was also announced at Made on YouTube in Sept 2025. — [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/youtube-title-a-b-testing-rolls-out-globally-to-creators/562571/); [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/youtube-adds-title-testing-youtube-studio/753015/); [Google blog – Made on YouTube 2025](https://blog.google/products/youtube/made-on-youtube-2025/)
- **Made on YouTube (Sept 2025)** announced "Ask Studio" (conversational analytics), the ability to **collaborate directly within Studio**, Veo 3 Fast inside Shorts, and podcast clipping tools. — [Google blog](https://blog.google/products/youtube/made-on-youtube-2025/); [Gigazine](https://www.gigazine.net/gsc_news/en/20250917-made-on-youtube-2025)
- **Podcasts:** YouTube reported more than **1B monthly active viewers of podcast content** (Jan 2025, announced 26 Feb 2025) and **over 400M hours of podcasts watched monthly on TVs** in 2024. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/02/26/youtube-surpasses-1-billion-monthly-podcast-viewers/); [Deadline](https://deadline.com/2025/02/youtube-podcasts-billion-monthly-active-viewers-streaming-1236301290/). A later report says **700M+ hours were watched on living-room devices in Oct 2025**, and Edison data shows **31% of weekly US podcast listeners prefer YouTube** over Spotify (27%) and Apple (15%). This is from secondary aggregators. — [Podcast Query](https://podcastquery.com/research/video-podcast-statistics); [Podder](https://www.podderapp.com/post/youtube-podcast-stats)

**Instagram Reels**
- Adam Mosseri said on **22 Jan 2025** that the three most important ranking signals are **watch time, likes per reach and sends (DM shares) per reach**. Likes matter slightly more for followers ("connected" reach) and sends matter slightly more for non-followers ("unconnected" reach). The "sends are worth 3–5 times a like" figure is a community estimate that Meta has not confirmed. — [Socialync](https://www.socialync.io/blog/adam-mosseri-shares-instagram-algorithm-2026); [HighStyle](https://www.highstyle.ai/insights/instagram-reels-algorithm-2026); [CreatorLane](https://creatorlanehq.com/blog/instagram-algorithm-2026-mosseri-vs-measured)
- Mosseri said in Feb 2025, as reported by Social Media Today, that Instagram looks at both the **percentage watched and the absolute seconds watched**, so longer videos are not penalised automatically. — [Socialync](https://www.socialync.io/blog/adam-mosseri-shares-instagram-algorithm-2026)
- **Reels can run up to 3 minutes since Jan 2025** (up from 90 s). Instagram also introduced a taller rectangular profile grid and launched the **Edits** video app. — [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/social-media/instagram-swoops-in-with-3-minute-reels-and-rectangular-profile-grids-as-the-tiktok-ban-gets-real-201316339.html); [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/technology/news/story/instagram-head-announces-big-changes-3-minute-reels-and-new-look-for-profile-grid-461527-2025-01-21)
- *Unverified:* "only Reels of 3 minutes or less are recommended to non-followers." No primary Instagram source was found. — [HighStyle](https://www.highstyle.ai/insights/instagram-reels-algorithm-2026)

**Facebook Reels and video**
- In **June 2025, Meta began treating all new Facebook videos as Reels**, renamed the Video tab "Reels" and removed the old length limit. Third-party data says **Reels over 1 minute make up about a quarter of Reels from creators with 10k+ followers but more than half of watch time**. This comes from secondary guides and was not confirmed with Meta. — [SocialPilot](https://www.socialpilot.co/blog/facebook-algorithm); [PostEverywhere](https://posteverywhere.ai/blog/how-the-facebook-algorithm-works)
- **Meta's originality rules (reported as March 2026):** content you film or produce yourself is original. Remixes count only when the creator appears and adds something new, such as analysis or commentary. Re-uploads with cosmetic changes such as borders, captions or speed tweaks are deprioritised. Accounts that repeatedly post unoriginal content can be marked non-recommendable and lose monetisation, with an appeal available. Meta reportedly says **views and time spent on original Reels roughly doubled in H2 2025 versus H2 2024**. — [ALM Corp](https://almcorp.com/blog/meta-original-content-rules-2026-facebook-instagram-creators/); [GadgetBond](https://gadgetbond.com/facebook-original-creators-win-update-2026/)
- Facebook reportedly updated Reels to surface **50% more same-day content**. — [SocialPilot](https://www.socialpilot.co/blog/facebook-algorithm)

**TikTok**
- **Creator Rewards Program** requirements: **videos must be longer than 1 minute**, the creator must be 18+, and the account needs ≥10k followers and ≥100k views in the last 30 days. **Pakistan is not on any published country list.** TikTok does not publish an official list, so check in TikTok Studio under Monetization. — [Epidemic Sound](https://www.epidemicsound.com/blog/how-to-make-money-on-tiktok/); [Metricool](https://metricool.com/tiktok-creator-fund/); [Toptal](https://www.toptal.com/creator/post/how-to-join-the-tiktok-creator-rewards-program)

**Captions and sound-off viewing (mostly older data, flagged)**
- A **2019** Verizon Media/Publicis study found up to **69% of consumers watch with sound off in public** and 25% do so even in private. Other secondary sources cite 92%. The often-repeated "85% of Facebook video plays without sound" comes from **2016-era Digiday** reporting. On TikTok the ratio reportedly flips, with about 80% watching with sound on. Meta tests reportedly showed captioned video ads increased view time by about 12%. All of these are dated or secondary. — [3Play Media (Verizon/Publicis)](https://www.3playmedia.com/blog/verizon-media-and-publicis-media-find-viewers-want-captions/); [Storyblocks](https://www.storyblocks.com/resources/blog/video-captioning); [PostEverywhere](https://posteverywhere.ai/social-media-terms/sound-on-vs-sound-off); [UTMB accessibility (Kapwing data)](https://www.utmb.edu/accessibility/digital/100-days-of-a11y/100-days-article/days-of-a11y/2024/04/25/day-18--most-people-use-closed-captions)

### Inferences
- **Lengths:** for dars-e-hadith or naseehat clips, 45–90 s Shorts/Reels is a reasonable default: long enough for one complete point with its reference, and in the range where vendor data shows high view totals. A complete 1–3 min clip should be used when the content holds attention, because all three platforms now accept up to 3 min and judge drop-off rather than length. TikTok monetisation also requires videos longer than 1 min, though it is not currently open to Pakistan.
- **Hooks:** "Viewed vs Swiped Away" (YouTube) and Instagram's watch time per reach make the first 1–3 s measurable. Opening with the question or problem (for example, "Does namaz break if…?") rather than salaams or an intro animation fits these signals. The project's intro card should be very short or come after the hook.
- **Shares and sends** are the strongest growth signal on Instagram for non-followers. Short, practical, quotable reminders that people forward on WhatsApp and DM suit Islamic content well.
- **Titles and thumbnails:** use Test & Compare on long-form uploads. Because the winner is chosen by watch-time share, a clickbait thumbnail that viewers abandon will lose the test.
- Burned-in captions are justified by sound-off viewing on Facebook (the most important platform in Pakistan) and by accessibility, even though the hard numbers are dated.
- Meta's originality rules support the project's model of original filmed lectures. Simply cross-posting someone else's clip with a border and captions would be penalised.

### Gaps
- **Posting cadence:** no official or credible 2025–26 study was found on the best posting frequency for any platform. Third-party claims were not searched exhaustively.
- **Collaborations:** YouTube's in-Studio collaboration feature (Sept 2025) is confirmed, but no data was found on its reach effect. Instagram Collab posts were not researched.
- **Series and playlists:** no current platform source was found quantifying the effect of playlists or series on watch time.
- No 2024–26 primary study of sound-off rates was found. Caption figures are from 2016–2021.
- No Islamic-content-specific benchmarks for retention or CTR were found.

## 3. Editing and visual trends among top educational and religious creators

### Takeaway
Good evidence on editing *styles* (kinetic captions, B-roll density, colour) is scarce. Most claims come from vendor blogs. The documented trends are structural: vertical first; long podcast-style recordings cut into clips; platform-native AI tools (Veo 3 Fast in Shorts, Instagram Edits, YouTube podcast clip tools); and platforms rewarding original, creator-on-screen material. AI-generated religious imagery has drawn documented backlash in Muslim-majority countries, which supports this project's rules of aniconic visuals and no realistic AI imagery.

### Cited Findings
- YouTube (Made on YouTube, Sept 2025) added **Veo 3 Fast in Shorts** (8-second AI clips with audio), "quick edits" for first drafts, **Speech to Song** (turns speech into a soundtrack), AI tools for **podcast clips**, and **auto-generated video for audio-only podcasts**. — [Google blog – Made on YouTube 2025](https://blog.google/products/youtube/made-on-youtube-2025/); [Gigazine](https://www.gigazine.net/gsc_news/en/20250917-made-on-youtube-2025)
- Instagram launched the **Edits** app, reported for release around March 2025, alongside 3-minute Reels and a vertical rectangular grid, a signal that full-frame 9:16 is the default. — [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/social-media/instagram-swoops-in-with-3-minute-reels-and-rectangular-profile-grids-as-the-tiktok-ban-gets-real-201316339.html); [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/technology/news/story/instagram-head-announces-big-changes-3-minute-reels-and-new-look-for-profile-grid-461527-2025-01-21)
- The **podcast format has grown into a main YouTube format**: 1B+ monthly podcast viewers and heavy TV viewing (see section 2). This underpins the "long conversation → many vertical clips" model. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/02/26/youtube-surpasses-1-billion-monthly-podcast-viewers/)
- Meta's originality rules reward **the creator appearing on screen and adding commentary**. Cosmetic edits to others' footage, such as borders, captions or speed changes, do not count as original. — [ALM Corp](https://almcorp.com/blog/meta-original-content-rules-2026-facebook-instagram-creators/)
- **AI backlash, Indonesia and Malaysia (June 2025):** AI-generated "First Day in Hell" videos were condemned by **MUI** (Indonesia's ulema council), which said they misrepresent religious doctrine and mislead younger viewers. There were calls for legal action. — [OECD AI Incident record](https://oecd.ai/en/incidents/2025-06-07-fd19)
- **Deepfakes of scholars:** a 2023 GNET report described a coordinated network using AI video and audio deepfakes, including **deepfakes of prominent Islamic scholars**, to promote a self-proclaimed "Mahdi" figure. — [GNET](https://gnet-research.org/2023/08/29/deepfake-doomsday-the-role-of-artificial-intelligence-in-amplifying-apocalyptic-islamist-propaganda/)
- Academic view: Christoph Günther (Erfurt) observes that Muslim creators use AI imagery to illustrate Qur'anic passages and hadith, blending sacred narrative with non-Islamic visual frameworks, and frames this as a modern continuation of illustration traditions. — [University of Erfurt, Islamic Studies news](https://www.uni-erfurt.de/en/philosophische-fakultaet/seminare-professuren/religionswissenschaft/professuren/islamic-studies/news)
- Vendor guidance on Shorts says creators should make two cuts (about 15–25 s and about 30–45 s) and compare retention. — [OpusClip blog](https://www.opus.pro/blog/ideal-youtube-shorts-length-format-retention); [Miraflow](https://miraflow.ai/blog/how-long-should-youtube-shorts-be-2026)
- Commercial nasheed libraries warn that "free nasheed" downloads can trigger claims weeks later. This is promotional, but it is a sound-design risk signal. — [Aswati](https://aswati.co/nasheed-for-youtube)

### Inferences
- A format of **talking head plus on-screen references** (ayah or hadith card with source), burned-in word-highlight captions, punch-in zooms and geometric or architectural backgrounds fits all the confirmed signals: original creator-on-screen content, sound-off viewing on Facebook, and avoiding the AI-imagery risk. This matches the project's existing `dai-video-editor` pipeline.
- **Realistic AI depictions (people, the Unseen, Jannah/Jahannam scenes) carry high reputational risk** with Muslim audiences, as the MUI condemnation shows. They also trigger AI labelling (section 4). Abstract geometry, light and architecture avoid both problems.
- Music-free sound design (voice plus room tone or subtle non-instrumental textures) avoids nasheed or music Content ID claims and fits audience preferences in this niche. No survey data was found to quantify that preference.

### Gaps
- No credible data was found on how kinetic captions, B-roll density, cinematic colour grading or motion graphics affect retention specifically for religious or educational content. Claims in vendor blogs were not quantified with published methods.
- No survey of Muslim audiences' attitudes to AI-generated visuals in religious content was found (Pakistan or diaspora). The backlash evidence is case-based.
- No data was found comparing vertical-native recording with reframed horizontal footage for this niche.
- No source was found on fatwas or official scholarly statements on AI depictions of prophets (for example from Al-Azhar or the Council of Islamic Ideology).

## 4. Policies relevant to Islamic creators: AI disclosure, religious content and hate speech, monetisation, Qur'an/nasheed copyright, Pakistan regulation

### Takeaway
All three major platforms require disclosure of *realistic* AI or synthetic media, and from 2026 they increasingly label it automatically (YouTube auto-labels, TikTok C2PA auto-labels that cannot be removed, Meta's "AI info"). YouTube's July 2025 "inauthentic content" rule demonetises mass-produced or repetitive content, not AI use as such. Religious subject matter is monetisable on YouTube; attacks on people based on religion and extremist or controversial-conflict content are not. In Pakistan, the PECA amendments of January 2025 created a new social-media regulator and a "false information" offence carrying up to 3 years in prison. Facebook monetisation for Pakistan is now invite-only apart from Stars, and TikTok Creator Rewards is not available in Pakistan.

### Cited Findings
**AI and synthetic content disclosure**
- **YouTube:** creators must disclose realistic altered or synthetic content that viewers could mistake for a real person, place or event, through the AI question in the upload flow (sources date the requirement to 18 Mar 2024 or 21 May 2025). From about **late May 2026, YouTube auto-labels undisclosed photorealistic AI video**: below the player on long-form, as an overlay on Shorts. Repeated non-disclosure can lead to removal or loss of YPP. **Likeness detection** is available to all eligible 18+ creators as of 19 May 2026; voice detection has been announced but is not live. Disclosure does not reduce reach or monetisation. These are secondary sources. — [Kompozy](https://kompozy.io/news/youtube-ai-disclosure-likeness-rules-2026); [WindowsForum](https://windowsforum.com/news/youtube-ai-disclosure-rules-label-realistic-deepfakes-in-2026.439959/); [AIR Media-Tech](https://air.io/en/youtube-hacks/youtube-ai-policy-2026-likeness-detection-and-the-no-fakes-act-what-creators-need-to-know); [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/youtube-adds-4-dispute-categories-for-ai-likeness-claims-that-block-videos/)
- **YouTube "inauthentic content" (15 Jul 2025):** the YPP "repetitious content" policy was renamed and clarified. It targets **mass-produced or repetitive content** such as templated videos with small changes and slideshows with identical voice-over, not AI use itself. Rene Ritchie said reaction and clip-based videos were not targeted. — [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/youtube-clarifies-monetization-update-inauthentic-repeated-content/752892/); [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/technology/youtube-updates-monetisation-policies-ai-and-repetitive-content-ban-begins-july-15-1.500192660); [AlternativeTo](https://alternativeto.net/news/2025/7/youtube-updates-its-policy-to-demonetize-inauthentic-mass-produced-ai-generated-content)
- **Meta:** since 2024, Meta labels AI images it detects through industry-standard markers. Users **must disclose photorealistic video or realistic-sounding audio** made with AI, and penalties may apply. The label was renamed from "Made with AI" to **"AI info"** after photographers objected to minor edits being flagged. — [UPI (Feb 2024)](https://www.upi.com/Top_News/US/2024/02/06/5651707229314/); [PhoneArena](https://www.phonearena.com/news/meta-changes-ai-generated-content-label_id160026); [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/meta-updates-ai-labeling-policy-to-expose-generated-content-in-its-apps/712497/)
- **TikTok:** TikTok reads **C2PA Content Credentials** at upload and **auto-applies an "AI-generated" label that cannot be removed**. Creators must also manually label realistic AI content. Sources disagree on scale (1.3B vs 3B videos labelled) and on when C2PA reading began (May 2024 vs Jan 2025). — [TikTok Newsroom](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-us/partnering-with-our-industry-to-advance-ai-transparency-and-literacy); [Storrito](https://storrito.com/resources/tiktoks-2026-ai-labeling-rules-and-what-they-signal-for-platform-governance/); [Pixo](https://pixo.video/blog/tiktok-ai-label-compliance-guide); [Kompozy](https://kompozy.io/guides/tiktok-ai-labeling-at-scale)

- YouTube's advertiser-friendly guidelines **bar ads on content that promotes discrimination or disparages people based on religion** (among other traits). This targets attacks on groups, not religious subject matter. Content on **war, political conflicts, terrorism or extremism** is generally ad-restricted, and recent tragedies may be ad-ineligible even in news or documentary framing. Limited ads does not mean removal. — [Subscribr ad-friendly checklist](https://subscribr.ai/youtube-strategy/youtube-ad-friendly-content-checklist); [MediaPost](https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/389685/youtube-updates-controversial-topic-ad-guidelines.html)

Child abuse, trafficking and eating disorders remain restricted. — [ABC News / AP](https://abcnews.go.com/Technology/wireStory/youtube-relaxes-monetization-policy-videos-controversial-content-129291801)
- One claim, unconfirmed: a Christian ministry reported a revenue fall of about 70% after its videos were flagged and alleged bias against religious and conservative content. — [Christian Post](https://assets.christianpost.com/news/is-youtube-targeting-christians-and-conservatives-with-its-advertising-policy-198744/?page=2)
- **TikTok in Pakistan:** TikTok removed about **24.95M Pakistani videos in Q1 2025**, **25.45M in Q2 2025** and **22.99M in Q4 2025** for guideline violations, with over 99% removed proactively and about 96–98% within 24 hours. Authorities have **banned TikTok several times since 2020**. — [Express Tribune](https://tribune.com.pk/story/2557451/1); [Arab News](https://www.arabnews.com/node/2619575/amp); [APP](https://www.app.com.pk/national/tiktok-removes-175-million-videos-globally-in-q4-2025/)

**Copyright: Qur'an recitation and nasheeds**
- **Content ID** matches uploads against recordings that rights-holders have registered. A claim depends on whether *that specific recording* is registered, not on the subject matter. — [Wikipedia, Content ID](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Content_ID)
- "Copyright-free Qur'an" channels (NCQ, CFQ) permit use **on condition of crediting the reciter or source and linking back**, and say omitting credit can bring a claim. These are the channels' own terms, not legal guarantees. — [NCQ – YouTube](https://m.youtube.com/c/NCQNoCopyrightQuran); [Copyright Free Quran Recitation](https://sites.google.com/view/copyrightfreequranrecitation/home/)
- Famous reciters' recordings (for example Mishary Alafasy, whose nasheeds have 200M+ YouTube views) are commercially significant. Who holds the rights was not established. — [Wikipedia, Mishari Alafasy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mishari_bin_Rashid_Alafasy)

**Pakistan regulation (PECA / PTA)**
- **The PECA (Amendment) Act 2025 (Jan 2025)** created the **Social Media Protection and Regulatory Authority (SMPRA)**, empowered to register platforms and block or direct removal of content. A Sept 2025 bill proposed ending intermediaries' liability exemptions. — [Express Tribune](https://tribune.com.pk/story/2523924/govt-moves-to-tame-social-media); [DISA](https://disa.org/pakistans-parliament-enacts-legislation-implementing-broad-social-media-regulations/); [NCHR report (Feb 2026)](https://nchr.gov.pk/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/NCHR-Report-on-PECA-and-the-2025-Amendments-Act.pdf)
- **Section 26A** makes it a crime to intentionally spread information known or believed to be **false or fake** and likely to cause fear, panic or unrest. The penalty is **up to 3 years in prison and/or a fine of up to Rs2M**. "False" and "fake" are undefined. **689 cases were registered from Jan to Aug 2025**, including 9 against journalists, and the NCCIA investigates. The PFUJ and HRCP have challenged the section in the Islamabad High Court; the outcome was not found. — [Journalism Pakistan](https://www.journalismpakistan.com/section-26a-of-peca-explained-what-you-need-to-know); [LUMS SAHSOL](https://sahsol.lums.edu.pk/node/25604); [RSIL](https://rsilpak.org/2025/2025-amendments-to-the-prevention-of-electronic-crimes-act-2016-an-introduction/)
- The amended law also targets content deemed contrary to **"the ideology of Pakistan"**. RSF reports that at least 9 journalists have been prosecuted and **about a dozen YouTube channels targeted**. — [RSF](https://rsf.org/en/pakistan-multiple-journalists-and-dozen-youtube-channels-targeted-under-peca-less-year)
- The **PTA processed 645,329 URLs for blocking and blocked 495,747** between 1 Jun 2021 and 30 Jun 2026. Blasphemy has been a core blocking criterion since the Inter-Ministerial Committee began directing the PTA, alongside pornography and later political speech. — [Journalism Pakistan](https://www.journalismpakistan.com/pta-blocked-nearly-496-000-urls-in-five-years); [Wikipedia, Internet censorship in Pakistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internet_censorship_in_Pakistan)

### Inferences
- **For this channel:** keep visuals non-realistic (geometry, light, calligraphy). That avoids mandatory AI labels on YouTube, Meta and TikTok as well as backlash. If any realistic AI element is ever used, disclose it. Never generate a synthetic voice or face of the aalim himself or of other scholars; likeness and deepfake risk cuts both ways.
- Editing each lecture individually into clips counts as original content under both YouTube's inauthentic-content rule and Meta's originality rules. The risk comes from churning near-identical template clips, for example the same quote card with a new background.
- **PECA 26A and blasphemy law make sourcing discipline a legal safeguard as well as a scholarly one.** Exact Qur'an and hadith references with grading, avoiding sectarian attacks on named groups or individuals, and avoiding claims about current events or panic topics reduce the exposure. This matches the CLAUDE.md requirement to verify references.
- **Recitation audio:** the safest option is the aalim's own recitation, or recordings with an explicit licence and the required credit. This fits the project's no-instrumental rule. Nasheeds need the same licence check.
- **Revenue for a Pakistan-based da'i:** YouTube (YPP, with Shorts revenue share on engaged views) is the main ad-revenue route. Facebook monetisation depends on an invitation plus Stars. TikTok is reach-only for now. Diaspora viewers greatly raise YouTube RPM.

### Gaps
- Primary policy pages (YouTube Help, Meta Transparency Center, TikTok Support) were not opened. Details come from secondary sources, and exact policy wording and dates (for example the start of YouTube auto-labelling) should be verified.
- No platform policy was found that *specifically* addresses Qur'an recitation in Content ID, and no data on how often recitation or nasheed claims happen.
- No case was found of PECA 26A being applied to *religious scholars'* content specifically. The SMPRA's operational status in late 2026 is unclear.
- Meta and TikTok rules on religious hate speech were not researched separately (only YouTube's ad-suitability rules were).
- No data was found on X's creator monetisation for Pakistan, or on podcast-platform monetisation (Spotify Partner Program) for Pakistan.

## 5. Multilingual strategy: multi-language audio, auto-dubbing, Urdu-English bilingual channels, Hindi/Urdu overlap

### Takeaway
YouTube auto-dubbing is now open to all creators in 27 languages (early 2026). **Urdu and Arabic videos can be auto-dubbed into English**, but dubbing *from* English is limited to about 20 languages, and the tone-preserving "Expressive Speech" mode covers 8 languages that do not include Urdu or Arabic. Separately, creators can upload their own multi-language audio tracks (up to about 30 per video, per one source). Spoken Urdu is understood by Hindi speakers, which opens Indian audiences to spoken content, though on-screen text in Nastaliq script is not readable for them.

### Cited Findings
- **Auto-dubbing is available to all creators in 27 languages** (YouTube blog, early 2026). In December (2025), **more than 6M daily viewers watched at least 10 minutes of auto-dubbed content**. Creators can switch auto-dubs off or upload their own tracks, and "smart filtering" skips music and silent content. — [YouTube Blog](https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/youtube-auto-dubbing-expressive-speech/); [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/youtube-auto-dubbing-tops-6m-daily-viewers-expands-to-27-languages/); [India TV](https://www.indiatvnews.com/technology/news/youtube-auto-dubbing-feature-expands-to-all-users-now-supports-27-languages-2026-02-05-1029070)
- **Direction matters:** videos in **Arabic, Bengali, Hindi, Urdu, Turkish and others can be auto-dubbed *into English***. **Dubbing from English is supported for about 20 languages**, including Hindi and Bengali. Urdu is not confirmed as a target language. — [FoneArena](https://www.fonearena.com/blog/474902/youtube-auto-dubbing-27-languages-expressive-speech-viewer-controls-lip-sync.html/amp); [TBS News](https://www.tbsnews.net/tech/youtube-rolls-out-auto-dubbing-globally-expanded-language-support-1353236)
- **Expressive Speech** (keeps the speaker's tone) supports only English, French, German, Hindi, Indonesian, Italian, Portuguese and Spanish. **Lip-sync** is still a pilot with selected channels; it was announced in May 2025 and enhanced at Made on YouTube in Sept 2025. — [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/technology/media/youtube-expands-ai-auto-dubbing-to-27-languages-with-expressive-speech-1.500433271); [Google blog](https://blog.google/products/youtube/made-on-youtube-2025/)
- An Aug 2026 guide claims auto-dubbing supports up to 30 languages but does not list them. — [MakeInfluence](https://www.makeinfluence.com/en/academy/youtube-auto-dubbing-aloud-and-why-danish-isnt-supported-yet)
- **Multi-language audio tracks** (creator-supplied dubs) are a separate feature, described as allowing up to 30 languages per video. — [Metricool](https://metricool.com/youtube-multi-language-audio-tracks-now-available-for-more-creators/)
- Hindi and Urdu are **mutually intelligible in colloquial speech** but differ in script and in formal (Perso-Arabic vs Sanskritised) vocabulary. India has about 50.8M Urdu mother-tongue speakers (2011 census). — [Wikipedia, Urdu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urdu)

### Inferences
- **Urdu lectures can get English dubs automatically.** For a scholar, the risk is mistranslation of Qur'anic or hadith terms and fiqh rulings. Review auto-dubs, or turn them off for masail videos, where precision matters most. Arabic quotations (ayat) inside an Urdu talk may be handled badly by the dubbing.
- **Indian reach:** plain spoken Urdu reaches Hindi speakers. Caption layers in Urdu (Nastaliq) plus Hindi (Devanagari) or Roman Urdu would widen access, and the project's skill already supports Hindi captions. Avoiding very Persianised vocabulary in short clips would also help.
- **English Muslim audiences:** a separate English-subtitled or English-dubbed track (auto-dub with review, or human voice-over) on the same video keeps watch time on one URL, rather than splitting it across a second channel.

### Gaps
- No published data was found comparing bilingual (Urdu-English) channels with separate-language channels for reach or retention.
- The full 27/30-language list was not found, so whether **Urdu is a dubbing *target*** (English → Urdu) is unconfirmed.
- No data was found on how often Indian viewers watch Pakistani Urdu religious content, or on any India-specific restrictions on Pakistani channels. India has blocked some Pakistani YouTube channels in the past, but this was not researched here.
- No data was found on Meta or TikTok auto-translation or dubbing features for Urdu.

## 6. Repurposing systems: one long lecture → clips, reels, quote cards, articles, newsletter

### Takeaway
The platforms now support a "pillar → clips" workflow natively: YouTube podcast-clip AI and auto-video for audio podcasts, Shorts linked to long videos, 3-minute Reels and Shorts, and Instagram Edits. Third-party clippers such as OpusClip are widely used. Both YouTube's inauthentic-content rule and Meta's originality rules penalise mass-produced near-duplicates and lightly altered re-uploads, so each derivative needs genuine editorial work: a clear standalone point, its own hook, and its own captions or references.

### Cited Findings
- YouTube (Sept 2025) launched **AI tools for podcast creators to make clips** and to **generate video for audio-only podcasts**. — [Google blog – Made on YouTube 2025](https://blog.google/products/youtube/made-on-youtube-2025/)
- YouTube video podcasts reach **1B+ monthly viewers**, much of the viewing happens on TVs, and the 1B figure includes clips and explainers. This supports long-form (TV) plus clips (mobile) as complementary formats. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/02/26/youtube-surpasses-1-billion-monthly-podcast-viewers/); [Podcast Query](https://podcastquery.com/research/video-podcast-statistics)
- The YouTube **inauthentic content** policy cites as risks "template videos with tiny changes", "slideshows with identical voice-over" and "copied content across your whole channel". Clip-based and reaction content with added value is not targeted. — [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/youtube-clarifies-monetization-update-inauthentic-repeated-content/752892/); [AlternativeTo](https://alternativeto.net/news/2025/7/youtube-updates-its-policy-to-demonetize-inauthentic-mass-produced-ai-generated-content)
- Under Meta's rules, **content you film or produce yourself is original**, while re-uploads with borders, captions or speed changes are unoriginal. — [ALM Corp](https://almcorp.com/blog/meta-original-content-rules-2026-facebook-instagram-creators/)
- Clipping vendors such as OpusClip publish guidance on making several cut lengths per idea and comparing retention, which shows these tools are standard practice. They are commercial sources. — [OpusClip blog](https://www.opus.pro/blog/ideal-youtube-shorts-length-format-retention)
- Instagram's **Edits** app and 3-minute Reels support native vertical editing. — [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/social-media/instagram-swoops-in-with-3-minute-reels-and-rectangular-profile-grids-as-the-tiktok-ban-gets-real-201316339.html)
- Pakistani brands are moving ad budgets toward creators and paying for performance (sales or commission) rather than reach (2026 headline). This is relevant to sponsorships of long lectures and series. — [PhoneWorld](https://www.phoneworld.com.pk/influencer-marketing-pakistan-performance-deals-creators-commission/)

### Inferences
- A practical pipeline for this project: record one 16:9 dars of 20–40 min, which serves as the YouTube long-form, podcast (audio plus video) and TV viewing. Cut 4–8 vertical clips of 45–120 s, each with its own hook and ayah or hadith card, for Shorts, Reels and TikTok, posted natively rather than cross-posted with watermarks. Add 1–2 quote cards or carousels with the exact reference, and a short Urdu text summary for the YouTube community tab or Facebook post. Optionally, an English-dubbed track on the long-form.
- Each clip should differ in substance (a different point or question), not only in background or template. That keeps it clear of both "inauthentic" and "unoriginal" penalties.
- Linking each Short to its full lecture (YouTube's related-video link) and grouping lectures into a series playlist is the natural way to convert short-form reach into long-form watch time. No quantitative source confirmed the effect.

### Gaps
- No credible data was found on how articles, newsletters or WhatsApp channels contribute to growth for religious creators. Pakistan-specific use of WhatsApp Channels was not researched.
- No independent benchmark was found comparing clipping tools (OpusClip, Descript, CapCut, YouTube native) on accuracy for Urdu or Arabic, especially auto-captions in Nastaliq script.
- No data was found on the reach penalty for cross-posting watermarked clips (for example TikTok watermarks on Reels) under the 2025–26 rules. The originality rules suggest risk, but it is not quantified.
