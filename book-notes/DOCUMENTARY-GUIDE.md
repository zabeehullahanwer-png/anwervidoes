# Book documentary guide: «ایک کتاب، ایک نشست»

## Purpose
The series conveys **Maulana Abul Hasan Ali Nadwi's own message**. It is not a book review, and it is not our personal sermon.
- The presenter acts as the author's narrator (راوی), saying "مولانا ندوی کہتے ہیں…".
- Our own commentary is kept to one short bridge per episode at most. It must follow the author's line of thought.

## Visual rules (fixed)
- **No pictures of any person**: no Prophet, Companion, scholar or figure, real or AI-generated. No AI "historical" faces or silhouettes of people either.
- **Animation is required**, but only of inanimate things:
  - maps of regions and routes
  - timelines
  - architecture
  - manuscripts and book pages
  - light and darkness effects
  - coins and treasuries
  - geometric patterns
- People and journeys are shown as **lines, lights and lamps on a map**, never as figures.
- Quotes from the book appear on screen with the Urdu page number.
- Ayat appear on a verse card with the exact mushaf text, the surah name and the ayah number.
- No instrumental music. Use ambient sound only.

## Episode plan (7 episodes plus one compiled film)
Each episode runs 6–8 minutes and follows the book's chapter order.

| # | Title | Book |

| 1 | اندھیری دنیا | Ch. I (pp. 37–99) and the author's intro |
| 2 | روشنی: نئی انسانیت | Ch. II (pp. 100–154) |
| 3 | قیادت کا دور | Ch. III (pp. 155–188) |
| 4 | زوال | Ch. IV (pp. 189–232) |
| 5 | مغرب کی قیادت | Ch. V (pp. 233–334) |
| 6 | انسانیت نے کیا کھویا | Ch. VI (pp. 335–382) |
| 7 | پیغام: عالمِ اسلام کی ذمہ داری | Ch. VII–VIII (pp. 383–450) |

After episode 7, join all episodes into one full film (about 45–50 min). Also cut 1–2 reels per episode.

## Script template (every episode)
1. **Opening (0:00–0:40).** Open with an image or story the author himself uses in that chapter, then state the chapter's question.
2. **The author's thesis.** One sentence: what Nadwi is proving in this chapter.
3. **The author's argument in his order.** Give 3–4 movements, each with one visual. Use at most 2 book quotes and 1 ayah card.
4. **The author's conclusion.** Close on his own closing words or ayah.
5. **Bridge (≤ 30 sec).** Explain why this chapter matters to his whole question («ماذا خسر العالم»), then tease the next episode.
