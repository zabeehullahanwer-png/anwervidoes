"Consideration of 'Urf in AAOIFI Sharī'ah Standards: An Analytical and Applied Study of Selected Standards"
AL-IMAN Research Journal, Vol. 3, Issue 4 (Oct–Dec 2025) · HEC-recognized (Y category) · Urdu

Islamic finance does not operate in a vacuum. Contracts, markets and business practices change with time and place, and classical Islamic jurisprudence has a principle for exactly this: 'Urf, recognized custom and practice.

This study examines how AAOIFI, the leading standard-setting body for Islamic financial institutions, takes 'Urf into account in selected Sharī'ah Standards: where custom shapes a ruling, how far it is allowed to, and what this means for practice in Islamic banks today.

I am grateful to my teachers and to the editorial team of AL-IMAN.
