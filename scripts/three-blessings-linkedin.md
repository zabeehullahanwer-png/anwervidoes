# LinkedIn post: "Learn to Live in the Present Moment"

## Post text

You might already have the whole world, and not know it.

Fourteen centuries ago, the Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him) said:

"Whoever among you wakes up secure in his home, healthy in his body, and has his food for the day, it is as if the whole world has been gathered for him."
(Jami' al-Tirmidhi)

Peace. Health. Today's provision.

In today's race to get ahead, many of us live everywhere except here. We plan the future. We replay the past: old mistakes, regrets, a quiet sense of guilt. The present moment, the one we actually own, slips by unnoticed.

Three reflections I keep coming back to:

1. Ambition is good. Ambition without gratitude becomes anxiety.
2. Regret should teach us, not trap us.
3. Calm comes from noticing what we already have.

Before your next meeting, take ten seconds and ask yourself: am I safe, am I well, do I have enough for today?
If the answer is yes, you are richer than you think.

What is one blessing you are grateful for today?
